What is Punktoidea?

Spelled like Punk to Idea, we are not nearly that cool.

Punctoidea: a superfamily of air-breathing land snails and slugs, terrestrial pulmonate gastropod mollusks in the informal group Sigmurethra.

Punktoidea: a regular family of air-breathing land humans, terrestrial humans in the informal group of friends.

We named it after a slug.
